        Stanford has videos online where Knuth presents and discusses \TEX\
        development. Watching them brings me back to school:

        \startitemize[packed]

                artificial intelligence was discussed: expert systems (prolog)
            \stopitem
            \startitem
                there were experiments with computer assisted teaching:

            \stopitem

                there was little clue how to apply computers in education (the apple

            \stopitem
            \startitem

                tapes (random access), simulations
            \stopitem
            \startitem
                computers were not yet everywhere, there was little interest, and
                it was time consuming
            \stopitem
        \stopitemize
